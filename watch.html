Learning to fly at API

Attitude Flying, our syllabus, and the training app that shows you exactly where you stand, from your first lesson to your checkride.

Transcript

You've watched the airplanes cross the Chicago sky. At Aviation Professionals, you learn to fly one.

We're a family-owned flight school at Chicago Midway, teaching people to fly since 1975. The oldest flight school in Chicago.

And we teach a method you won't find anywhere else: Attitude Flying. You fly the airplane by what you see out the window, not by chasing needles on the panel.

The nose, the strut, the wingtip and the windowsill are your instruments. Set the picture, trim off the pressure, and the airplane flies itself while you look outside.

Two rules never change. Throttle controls airspeed. The control wheel controls altitude. Learn those, and every maneuver after builds on them.

Every API student also gets our training app, on your phone and your computer, from your very first lesson.

Open it and you see exactly where you stand: your phase, your percentage complete, and what comes next.

Your training moves through seventeen flight phases in four stages, each one built on the last, from your first taxi to your checkride.

After every flight, your instructor marks each standard met or not yet. You always know what you nailed, and what to work on next time.

Every skill lines up with the FAA's Airman Certification Standards, the same standards your examiner uses. Watch your ACS coverage fill in as you train, so checkride day holds no surprises.

Your full API syllabus lives in the app: the attitude flying references, the traffic pattern, the landing profiles and every maneuver, all illustrated and ready to study before you fly.

Each phase shows what you'll fly, the pictures to study, and what a good flight looks like, so you show up prepared.

Your digital logbook keeps every flight, signed by your instructor, with your totals always up to date. Your endorsements are right there too. Got a question at midnight? Ask Bill, our training assistant. He knows your record and the syllabus, and he'll even read the answer aloud.

A proven method, a family that cares, and a clear view of your progress, from first flight to checkride.
